Presentation adds higher sampling, contact shadows, a matte stage and environment reflections. It renders on demand when still. Turntable motion pauses while you inspect and resumes only when requested.

This is rasterized real-time rendering, not a path tracer. Available component models determine assembly fidelity; missing models remain visibly reported as proxies. The display and controls demo uses scripted virtual overlays.
